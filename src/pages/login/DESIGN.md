## Brand & Style

The design system expresses a serene, sanctuary-inspired aesthetic tailored for high-end beauty, dermatology, and wellness clients. It bridges the precision of contemporary clinical care with the tactile warmth of an editorial lifestyle atelier.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Drivers
- **Tactile Serenity:** The interface breathes through expansive, warm ivory negative space, inviting calm rather than haste during appointment reservations.
- **Editorial Elegance:** High-contrast serif headlines evoke luxury editorial publishing, anchoring services with an elevated sense of bespoke craftsmanship.
- **Quiet Reassurance:** Microcopy and booking touchpoints emphasize clarity, transparent deposits, and low-friction interactions to cultivate client trust.

### Visual Philosophy
Rooted in high-fashion editorial styling and Morandi still-life tonality:
- Architectural structural cards with controlled, refined radii.
- Soft, muted rose-plum anchors paired with warm alabaster backgrounds.
- Ultra-fine, hairline borders rather than heavy, drop-shadowed boundaries.
- Monastic layout discipline: minimal surface decoration, intentional typographic scale, and ample visual pauses.

## Colors

The palette relies on muted, desaturated Morandi tones that provide high visual comfort without sacrificing accessibility or contrast.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#9B5D64` - Morandi Plum Rose):** The signature brand tone. Used for authoritative headline elements, primary interactive CTAs, key selected dates, and brand markers.
- **Secondary (`#D9777F` - Delicate Blush Rose):** Used for micro-accents, active reservation indicators, step highlights, and interactive focus states.
- **Tertiary (`#76877D` - Muted Sage):** A calming herbal accent reserved for positive confirmation states, available time slots, and serene highlights.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F5` / `#F4EFEB`):** Soft warm ivory and linen backdrops replacing sterile digital white, reducing visual fatigue and establishing tactile warmth.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#2C2526` - Espresso Charcoal):** Deep, warm charcoal providing optimal contrast for editorial serifs and body text without the harshness of pitch black.

### Functional Status Indicators
- **Available:** Light sage tint (`#EEF2EF`) with deep sage text (`#435349`) and tertiary dot indicators.
- **Limited Availability:** Soft apricot tint (`#FAF0E8`) with warm sienna text (`#8A4B29`).
- **Fully Booked:** Subdued warm linen (`#EFECE8`) with muted stone gray text (`#948E88`).

## Typography

Typography governs the tension between editorial romance and clinical utility. 

### Typographic Hierarchy
- **Headline Expression (`Bodoni Moda`):** Utilized exclusively for primary service category headers, confirmation milestones, treatment names, and hero greetings. Retains deliberate, high-contrast strokes that evoke luxury salon periodicals.
- **Functional Clarity (`Manrope`):** Handles service descriptions, pricing tables, slot pickers, metadata, and form inputs. Manrope’s geometric yet open structure maintains legibility at 12–14px sizes on mobile viewports.
- **Editorial Micro-Labels:** `label-md` and `label-sm` are always rendered in uppercase with slight tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to delineate metadata tags (e.g., "60 MIN", "DEPOSIT REQUIRED") cleanly against card backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adapts fluidly within a mobile-first paradigm, shifting gracefully to multi-column arrangements on larger viewports while retaining disciplined edge margins.

### Mobile Grid & Canvas (Up to 600px)
- **Grid Structure:** Single-column flow with a strict 4-column sub-grid for scheduling slots, therapist selectors, and duration chips.
- **Outer Canvas Margins:** Strict `1.25rem` (20px) horizontal inset, preventing touch targets from clipping display edges.
- **Rhythm:** `space-md` (16px) standard gap between card sections; `space-xl` (40px) thematic separation between treatment discovery and calendar configuration.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Adapters
- **Mobile (<640px):** Single-column stacked cards, full-width fixed reservation bottom sheet.
- **Tablet (640px–1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins; service browsing splits into a 2-column card grid, while calendar and deposit checkout utilize a dedicated side drawer.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a max-width of 1180px, maintaining an intimate, curated experience rather than excessive fluid sprawl.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain an organic, tactile luxury atmosphere, elevation relies on subtle tonal layering and delicate ambient diffusion rather than deep digital drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FAF7F5` (Alabaster Linen). Completely flat backdrop.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Panels):** Pure solid `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF8F7` combined with a hairline border: `1px solid rgba(155, 93, 100, 0.12)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Action Trays & Modal Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ambient tinted shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(44, 37, 38, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(155, 93, 100, 0.04);`
- **Tier 3 (Active Overlays & Calendar Popovers):** Hairline rim light `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` layered with soft Morandi blur:
  - `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -8px rgba(44, 37, 38, 0.12);`

### Delicacy Over Weight
Harsh black drops are forbidden. Elevation shadows must carry 4% to 12% opacity with warm espresso/plum tinting to preserve softness against neutral canvas tones.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances architectural discipline with soft, body-centric curves.

### Corner Curvature Scale
- **Base Components (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Form fields, treatment card containers, appointment overview modules, and step containers.
- **Secondary Containers (`1rem` / 16px):** Bottom reservation drawers, high-priority promotion panels, and salon media showcases.
- **Capsule / Pill Badge Geometry (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for dynamic status tags, duration chips ("45 MIN"), deposit tags, and category navigation pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Morandi Plum background (`#9B5D64`), white Manrope text (`label-md`), `0.5rem` border-radius, minimum 48px height. Hover/active states subtly shift to `#854E54` with subtle scale feedback (`scale(0.99)`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, `1px solid #9B5D64` border, `#9B5D64` text. Used for "View Details" or "Reschedule".
- **Floating Checkout Tray:** Stays pinned at the mobile viewport base with a subtle blur effect (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background: rgba(250, 247, 245, 0.92)`). Houses the deposit summary and instant "Confirm Reservation" button.

### Badges & Pill Chips
- **Service Tags:** Height 26px, padding `0 10px`, fully rounded pill (`9999px`).
  - *Deposit Tag:* Blush rose tint (`#F9EBEC`), text `#9B5D64`.
  - *Duration Tag:* Linen tint (`#F4EFEB`), text `#6E6667`.
- **Availability Chips:**
  - *Available:* Background `#EEF2EF`, border `1px solid #76877D`, text `#3D4C42`. Prefixed with a 6px tertiary sage circle.
  - *Limited Seats:* Background `#FAF0E8`, border `1px solid #E4AC8E`, text `#7D4122`.
  - *Fully Booked:* Background `#EFECE8`, border `1px solid #DED9D4`, text `#9C9691`, struck-through or faded.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Structure: White base (`#FFFFFF`), `0.5rem` corner radius, perimeter hairline `1px solid rgba(155, 93, 100, 0.12)`.
- Internal Layout: Treatment name in `headline-sm` (`Bodoni Moda`), duration and deposit pills aligned below, pricing right-aligned in `title-md`.
- Selected State: Surface shifts to warm linen tint, outline elevates to `1.5px solid #9B5D64`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Container height 52px, background `#FAF8F7`, subtle inset border `1px solid rgba(44, 37, 38, 0.12)`.
- Focused State: Border darkens to `#9B5D64` with no thick outer glow, only a crisp, subtle plum ring (`rgba(155, 93, 100, 0.15)`).

### Calendar & Time Slot Matrix
- Days displayed in an architectural horizontal ribbon. The active selected date is filled with solid `#9B5D64` with clean white typography.
- Time slots are modular pills arranged in 3 or 4 columns. Unavailable slots are muted with a soft opacity reduction (`0.45`).

### Confirmation & Reassurance States
- **Success Milestone:** Centered composition with a delicate, animated hairline circle enclosing a fine Morandi sage checkmark.
- **Deposit Summary Module:** Clean itemized receipt breakdown displaying service price, applied tax, and deposit deducted today, accompanied by a soothing microcopy guarantee: *"Free cancellation up to 24 hours prior to appointment."*
